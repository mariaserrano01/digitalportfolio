How it works

A portfolio project built around one question: which marketing openings deserve my time? Three sections. Job search filters by country first, then splits the screen into analytical roles (analytics, insights, growth, CRM, digital) and strategic roles (strategy, brand, operations, communications), each with its own role-type filter; work arrangement, job type, level, industry, pay and skills apply to both sides, and every card explains what the job is. Learn the areas describes the eight areas of marketing, grouped the same way: what people do, the methods and tools, the role types, where the jobs are and what to study. Dream Jobs is a shortlist ranked against your inputs. Any opening can be promoted to Dream Jobs with one click.

The page ships with a sample set: 36 openings and 55 Dream Jobs in the United States and Colombia, read on job boards and the employers' own careers pages and verified on the date in the footer. Add your own openings and areas, hide the sample, and export or import your data as JSON from My data.

Relevance score in Dream Jobs (0–100)

  • Resume match · 35 points. The posting's tools and skills (its named tools plus a lexicon of about 130 marketing, analytics and business skills found in its requirements) are checked against your resume text. Share found × 10. Neutral (5/10) until you add a resume.
  • Role fit · 25 points. How close the job is to the work you want, on a 0–10 scale: an editorial rating of analytical and strategic content for the sample, your own rating for openings you add, neutral (5) for openings promoted from Job search.
  • Your interest · 25 points. Your own 1–10 rating in each posting's detail view. Neutral (5/10) until you rate it.
  • Salary compatibility · 15 points. 10 when the published range starts at or above your target, 7 when it overlaps your minimum, 2 when its top is below your minimum, 5 when pay is not published or no target is set. Targets are per currency: USD per year for the United States, COP per month for Colombia.

What the page does not do

  • It does not search the web or refresh itself. The sample is a snapshot; a posting marked open may have closed since.
  • It does not send your resume, targets or openings anywhere. The resume text lives in memory only and is gone when you reload; targets, openings, areas, favorites, statuses, ratings and notes are kept in this browser's local storage.
  • The resume match is keyword-based: a triage aid, not a judgment of anyone's qualifications. Pay marked as an estimate is an estimate, not the employer's figure.

Sample data

Dream Jobs fields: company, title (with the original title when the posting is in Spanish), official URL, requisition ID, location and work arrangement, employment type, seniority, timing, industry, area and role type, published pay (currency, period, range, base vs total), the posting's own words on visa sponsorship, a paraphrased description, responsibilities, required and preferred qualifications and named tools. Job search fields: company, title, city, arrangement, industry, area and role type, level, pay (published or market estimate, with a note), skills, source and the explanations shown on each card. Descriptions are paraphrases, not posting text. Charts are plain HTML bars with a colorblind-checked palette and work in light and dark mode.
