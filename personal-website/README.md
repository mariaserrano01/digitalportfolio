# mariaserrano.co — personal site

**Live:** [mariaserrano.co](https://mariaserrano.co)

*"Analytics marketer. Open-water swimmer."* The site is the long-form version of this profile: who I am, what the work moved, and how I decide with data. It is written as one scrolling story with anchored sections.

## Sections

| Section | What it covers |

| **Water** | Open-water swimming and why hard things are worth loving: the Oceanman World Final in Dubai, 2024. |
| **Work** | Marketing at Óberon TSI, a risk-consulting firm: what the work moved, and the products I helped sell: Oberon 360 (live monitoring), LUNA (an AI assistant) and Oberon News (weekly risk alerts). |
| **Race** | *Abraza un sueño, corre con el corazón*: the charity race I created for Fundación Abraza un Sueño, for children with cancer and rare diseases. |
| **Approach** | Intuition is a hypothesis; data decides whether it ships. A four-step, data-driven method for testing marketing ideas. |
| **Now** | The Master in Marketing Analytics at UT Austin McCombs, the skills I am building and what I am building toward. |
| **Contact** | LinkedIn. |
